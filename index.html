About Us

Susi and Raji are two passionate business women from Namakkal district who started a small homemade food business with the goal of providing healthy and traditional foods to families.

We specialize in preparing healthy homemade podi and nutritious mixes using fresh and natural ingredients. Our products are prepared hygienically without preservatives.

We provide delivery services all over Tamil Nadu so customers can enjoy healthy homemade foods anywhere.
